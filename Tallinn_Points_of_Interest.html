Lower Old Town

Aug 14 — half day, arrival afternoon

The first half-day focuses entirely on the historic landmarks clustered around Lower Old Town and Town Hall Square.

12:00 – 2:00 PM

Free Tour of Tallinn Old Town

Show up a little before noon in front of the Tallinn Tourist Information Centre at Niguliste 2 to catch the 2-hour free guided walking tour. No booking required — every day at 12:00 PM.

2 hrs No booking needed Open in Maps
2:15 – 3:30 PM

Raeapteek & Town Hall Square

Step inside the historic Raeapteek (Town Hall Pharmacy) — the oldest continuously running pharmacy in Europe, and one of the most important buildings of Tallinn Old Town's UNESCO World Heritage Site, preserving its original function to this day. Spend a few minutes admiring the exterior of the medieval Town Hall.

Open in Maps

Town Hall Guided Tour

The best way to explore the fascinating history and rare artwork of the oldest town hall in Northern Europe. Book in advance — preferably 10 days ahead.

Katariina Käik (St. Catherine's Passage)

Stroll through this beautiful 14th-century cobblestone lane, believed to be several centuries old, winding from Vene Street to Müürivahe Street. Watch local glass, leather, and ceramic artists work in their studios.

Viru Gate & Flower Market

Walk through the fairytale towers of Viru Gate — built in the 14th century as part of the city's defenses and the main entrance to Tallinn Old Town — and browse the vibrant flower stalls lining its base.

1:15 – 2:15 PM

Toompea Castle — Free Tour

Head up the hill into the upper Old Town. One of the most historic tourist attractions in Tallinn, with a rich history dating back to the 13th and 14th centuries, lush gardens, and scenic walking trails. Now houses the Estonian parliament. Free guided tours normally run Monday–Thursday 10:00–15:00 and Fridays 10:00–13:00 — since August 14th is a Friday, tours end early that day, so visiting Saturday afternoon allows more time to explore the gardens, trails, and exterior at your own pace.

Open in Maps
2:15 – 3:30 PM

Alexander Nevsky Cathedral & Organ Music

Explore the stunning interior, with mosaics depicting religious figures and biblical stories. Entrance is free (donations welcome), open daily 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Since it's Saturday, stick around for the traditional half-hour organ performance beginning at 4:00 PM.

Free entry Organ music Sat/Sun 4:00 PM Open in Maps

Bogs & Coastal Parks

Aug 16 — bog hike morning, palaces & coast afternoon

The final day mixes a morning immersion into Estonia's unique bog culture with an afternoon exploring coastal palaces and museums.

7:00 – 11:30 AM

Viru Raba Bog Hike

Catch an early morning direct route on Bus 151 from Tallinn's central station out to Lahemaa National Park, roughly 45 minutes east of the city. Walk the wooden boardwalk trail over ancient moss fields and clear, amber-colored freshwater pools. Buses run hourly, earliest departure 5:50 AM, all direct with no transfers required.

Bus 151 · direct · hourly Open in Maps
12:15 – 2:00 PM

KGB Prison Cells

After arriving back in the city, dive into the darker history of the Soviet occupation. The basement of the building at 1 Pagari Street was home to one of the most notorious and feared pre-trial detention centers of the era.

Open in Maps
2:00 – 2:30 PM

Freedom Square

Walk down to view the Victory Column dedicated to Estonian independence — one of the city's most historic locations, next to the Old Town.

Open in Maps
2:30 – 4:30 PM

Kadriorg Park & Palace

Take public transit or a 30-minute walk east from the city center to stroll the manicured lawns of one of the nicest places to walk in Tallinn. Beautifully landscaped gardens, lush lawns, and well-maintained paths. View the grand Office of the President of the Republic of Estonia and the exterior of Kadriorg Palace.

Open in Maps
4:30 – 6:30 PM

Pirita Summer District

A short bus ride further northeast along the coastline — Tallinn's destination for summer fun and breathtakingly beautiful nature, just a few kilometres from the city centre and easily accessible by public transport. Walk along the sandy beach, enjoy the seaside atmosphere, and view the boat harbor.

Open in Maps
Public transit tip: Purchase a QR-ticket online or use a contactless bank card to tap onto Tallinn's highly efficient trams and buses — saves plenty of time getting out to Kadriorg Park and Pirita.
Lahemaa bus planning: Double-check the exact holiday-weekend schedule for Bus 151 at the central bus station (Tallinna Bussijaam) the day before — morning timetables occasionally shift slightly on summer Sundays.

Also Worth Knowing

Nõmme: a quieter residential district, home to Tallinn's best-known vegan restaurant scene — Vegan Restoran V and Veg Machine both worth seeking out.
