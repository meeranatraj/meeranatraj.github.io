Maritime Fortress & Architecture

Aug 18 — Suomenlinna, then a walking loop through the city
9:00 AM

Market Square & Old Market Hall

Start the morning at the waterfront. Grab a coffee and a fresh pastry at the orange outdoor market tents — also the best place in the city for reasonably priced wooden souvenirs.

Open in Maps
10:00 AM

Suomenlinna Sea Fortress

Catch the public HSL ferry from Market Square. An island that once served as a fortress — a UNESCO World Heritage site. Spend 3 hours walking through the tunnels, stone walls, and scenic coastal trails.

3 hrs on the island HSL ferry Open in Maps
1:15 PM — Lunch

Lunch at the Harbor

Take the ferry back to the mainland. Eat traditional salmon soup or fried Baltic herring at the Market Square stalls.

2:15 PM

Uspenski Cathedral

Turn left from the market and walk east into the Katajanokka neighborhood. The largest Orthodox church in Western Europe — tour the massive red-brick Russian Orthodox cathedral. Open Tuesday–Friday 9:30 AM – 4 PM.

€5 Tue–Fri 9:30–16:00 Open in Maps
3:15 PM

Central Train Station

Walk west past Market Square toward the city center. On the way, you'll pass Helsinki Cathedral again — worth a glance up at the roofline for the zinc statues of the twelve apostles, no need to go back inside. Stop at Helsinki Central Railway Station to admire Eliel Saarinen's world-famous Neoromantic architecture and the giant lantern-holding statues — one of the world's most beautiful railway stations.

Open in Maps
4:15 PM

Temppeliaukio (Rock Church)

Continue walking west to the Töölö neighborhood. A spectacular church hewn directly out of solid rock, topped with a glowing copper dome that floods the space with light through floor-to-ceiling windows.

Porvoo & Helsinki Museums

Aug 19 — day trip out, free museums back in the city
8:30 AM

Bus from Helsinki to Porvoo

Go to the underground bus terminal at Kamppi Center. Take a direct bus (OnniBus or Matkahuolto) — the ride takes exactly 50 minutes and drops off right next to the historic area.

OnniBus tickets Kamppi Center
9:30 AM

Historic Porvoo Old Town

Walk the 13th-century cobblestone streets, take photos of the iconic red riverside wooden warehouses, and step inside the medieval Porvoo Cathedral. Porvoo is the second-oldest town in Finland, incredibly well preserved and reflected through its unique architecture.

Open in Maps
11:00 AM

Castle Hill (Linnamäki) Nature Walk

Cross the pedestrian bridge over the river to Castle Hill. Walk the forested trails and elevated wooden boardwalks through the peaceful marshland nature reserve for a taste of authentic Finnish wilderness.

Open in Maps
12:30 PM — Lunch

Lunch in Porvoo

Eat at one of the cozy cafés or restaurants inside an old wooden building in the old town.

1:45 PM

Bus Back to Helsinki

Catch the return bus to Kamppi Center, arriving back in Helsinki around 2:35 PM.

3:00 PM

Helsinki City Museum

Finish the afternoon at this free museum to see how Helsinki transformed from a tiny wooden town into a modern capital. Closes at 7:00 PM.

Options — If There's Time

National Museum of Finland

The country's main museum — a great place to visit if the schedule allows. €12, open 11 AM – 6 PM.

Sipoonkorpi National Park Day Trip

Sits directly on the edge of Helsinki — a perfect taster into Finland's natural world. Take a bus from Helsinki to one of the nearby towns in or by the park, then a local taxi further into the area.
