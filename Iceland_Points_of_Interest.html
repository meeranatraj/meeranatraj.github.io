Reykjavík City Afternoon

Aug 5 — walking day, downtown & waterfront
2:00 – 3:00 PM

Hallgrímskirkja Church & Tower View

Iceland's tallest church — take the elevator up the tower for a rooftop panorama over Reykjavík's colorful streets.

3:00 – 4:15 PM

Rainbow Street & Downtown Stroll

Walk downhill along Skólavörðustígur (the Rainbow Street). Explore the local craft boutiques, woolen shops, and art galleries at a relaxed pace.

4:15 – 5:15 PM

Waterfront Walk & Harpa Concert Hall

Walk past The Sun Voyager shoreline sculpture and step inside Harpa Concert Hall. Enjoy the flat, easy-to-walk polished floors while looking through the geometric glass honeycomb facade.

Open in Maps
5:30 – 8:00 PM

The Perlan Museum Experience

Explore the Wonders of Iceland exhibitions. Walk through the world's first man-made indoor ice cave (built with 350 tons of real snow), watch the Áróra planetarium show, and step out onto the 360° observation deck to see the city in the bright August afternoon.

5,390 ISK (~$39 USD) / adult 20% discount, 67+ yrs Book in advance Open in Maps

Public bus: Walk 5 minutes from Harpa to the Lækjartorg bus hub. Catch Bus Line 18 heading toward Bústaðavegur. Step off at the Perlan stop, which leaves a short uphill walk through the surrounding park to the entrance.

Golden Circle

Aug 6 — self-drive loop, ~270 km round trip

Route source: adventures.is/iceland/attractions/golden-circle

Golden Circle route — attraction map
Þingvellir National Park
45 min drive1.5 hrs

Birthplace of Iceland's ancient parliament. The park's visitor center is located near the P1 parking lot.

Efstidalur Dairy Farm
25 min drive½ hr

Famous for its farm-to-table ice cream. Opens at 10 AM.

Haukadalur Geothermal Area
15 min drive1 hr

Great Geysir — erupting hot water high into the air every 4–10 minutes.

Gullfoss Falls
10 min drive1 hr · 2 km round trip

The upper parking lot is close to the Gullfoss Visitor Center, with restrooms, a souvenir shop, and a café. The Geysir Center across the road has restaurants, restrooms, a souvenir shop, and a hotel. Walking trail to the falls takes 15–30 minutes to complete.

Faxi Waterfall
9 min drive20 min

A beautiful, wide, cascading waterfall on the Tungufljót river.

Friðheimar Tomato Farm
10–15 min drive45 min

Family-run greenhouse that grows tomatoes year-round using geothermal energy.

Secret Lagoon
15 min drive2 hrs

Thermal hot spring — booked for 6:00 PM.

Kerið Crater
32 min drive30–40 min

Also called the "Eye of the World" — a stunning red volcanic rock crater.

Þjórsárdalur (optional)
49 min drive30 min

Háifoss is one of Iceland's most breathtaking waterfalls and, at 122 meters (400 feet), one of the tallest in the country.
